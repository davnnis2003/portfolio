# My Approach to Data Leadership

## Philosophy

I believe data teams succeed when they balance **three core responsibilities**:

1. **Deliver Business Value** — Analytics must drive decisions, not just reports
2. **Build Technical Foundation** — Quality data infrastructure enables scale
3. **Develop People** — Great teams outperform great individuals

## As a First Data Hire

When joining a company without an established data function, my first 90 days follow a clear pattern:

### Days 1-30: Assessment & Quick Wins
- **Understand the business** — Shadow stakeholders, learn key metrics, identify pain points
- **Audit existing data** — Map sources, assess quality, document gaps
- **Deliver first insights** — Build trust through immediate value

### Days 31-60: Foundation & Process
- **Establish architecture** — Implement modern data stack (ELT pattern)
- **Define operating model** — Set prioritization frameworks, meeting cadences, SLA expectations
- **Create documentation** — Make knowledge accessible and self-service

### Days 61-90: Scale & Enable
- **Build stakeholder enablement** — Train business users, create self-service dashboards
- **Implement quality framework** — Add testing, monitoring, alerting
- **Plan for growth** — Document what the next hire needs to succeed

## What Makes Me Effective

### Leadership Without Authority
I've built teams, influenced executives, and drove change without relying on hierarchical power. The key is **demonstrating value first**, then scaling through enablement.

### Technical Depth + Business Acumen
I can write dbt models, debug Airflow pipelines, and explain CAC calculations to the CFO. This dual fluency bridges the gap between engineering and business.

### Systems Thinking
I don't just solve today's problem—I design systems that prevent tomorrow's. Every process, model, and dashboard is built with scale in mind.

## My Non-Negotiables

- **Documentation is not optional** — If it's not documented, it doesn't exist
- **Quality over speed** — Technical debt compounds; better to build right than build fast
- **Stakeholder partnership** — We're business partners, not order-takers
- **Transparency** — Clear communication about capacity, trade-offs, and timelines

## What I'm Looking For

I'm most effective in environments where:
- Leadership values data-driven decision making
- There's appetite to invest in foundational work (not just quick fixes)
- Cross-functional collaboration is encouraged
- Technical excellence is celebrated
