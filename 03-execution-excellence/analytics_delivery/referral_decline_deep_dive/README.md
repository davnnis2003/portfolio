Referrals are an important source of new customer acquisitions for HelloFresh. We ask existing customers to refer a friend to us, and if that friend converts both receive discounts: the new customer off their first meal box and the referrer off their next meal box. Over the last year the performance of Referrals has declined, so the Product Owner of the domain has asked you as their Product Analyst to prepare a proposal for analysing the referrals conversions funnels to help the team identify opportunities to improve performance.

Please prepare a brief overview of how you would tackle the problem, to be presented in no more than 10 minutes \+ 5 minutes for questions. You may consider including the following:

* A high level overview of the customer flow through the referrals conversion funnels, including the data you anticipate would be available for the analysis.  
* A suggestion of some metrics that reflect the overall outcomes the team should be striving for. These might make sense for the team to adopt and track as primary metrics.  
* How you would break down the problem from an analytical perspective to identify where the biggest opportunities are in these flows to improve the customer experience and drive performance.  
* Some hypotheses for why performance may have been declining, together with how you might test these using the historical data available.

	You can present this proposal as you wish \- entirely verbally, using a presentation or other document, or in any other way. This task should not take you more than 2 hours. We won’t judge you on the style of your presentation template, but rather on your thinking and the clarity in your communication.
