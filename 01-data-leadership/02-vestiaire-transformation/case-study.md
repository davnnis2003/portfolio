# Case Study: Building a BI Squad from Scratch at Vestiaire Collective

**Industry:** Luxury Fashion eCommerce  
**Duration:** Nov 2021 - Present  
**Role:** Business Intelligence & Analytical Engineering Lead

---

## Executive Summary

Led the formation and growth of a Business Intelligence squad from inception, establishing analytics capabilities across Operations, Sustainability, Consignment, and Resale-As-A-Service domains. Implemented modern data stack, defined team processes, and drove stakeholder partnerships with senior leadership.

---

## Challenge

Vestiaire Collective needed dedicated analytics capabilities for critical business domains that were previously underserved:
- **No dedicated BI team** for Operations, Sustainability, and emerging business areas
- **Fragmented analytics** with inconsistent methodologies across teams
- **Limited data quality oversight** leading to trust issues
- **Growing stakeholder needs** without structured prioritization
- **Lack of documentation and standards** for data products

---

## Approach

### 1. Team Building & Formation
**Recruitment & Onboarding**
- Defined role requirements and competencies for BI squad members
- Led end-to-end recruitment process from job description to offer
- Created comprehensive onboarding program covering:
  - Technical stack (dbt, Snowflake, Tableau)
  - Business domain knowledge
  - Stakeholder landscape
  - Team processes and standards

**Mentorship & Development**
- Implemented regular 1-on-1 coaching sessions
- Created skill development plans for team members
- Facilitated knowledge sharing through pair programming
- Established code review practices for continuous learning

### 2. Strategic Stakeholder Partnerships
**Executive Alignment**
- Built relationships with senior leaders in Operations and Sustainability
- Established transparent communication channels
- Aligned analytics roadmap with business priorities
- Facilitated regular stakeholder reviews

**Cross-functional Collaboration**
- Coordinated with Data Engineering, Data Science, and Product Analytics teams
- Created shared understanding of data architecture and ownership
- Implemented collaboration frameworks for cross-team initiatives

### 3. Prioritization & Delivery Frameworks
**Implemented MoSCoW Framework**
- Must Have: Critical business metrics and operational dashboards
- Should Have: Enhanced analytics and exploratory analyses
- Could Have: Nice-to-have insights and experiments
- Won't Have: Clearly documented scope boundaries

**Quarterly Roadmap Planning**
- Aligned with business OKRs and strategic initiatives
- Balanced stakeholder requests with technical debt
- Created visibility into team capacity and commitments

**NOW/NEXT/LATER Backlog**
- NOW: Current sprint work and urgent requests
- NEXT: Planned for upcoming sprint
- LATER: Backlog of future initiatives

### 4. Agile Delivery & Documentation
**Scrum Implementation**
- 2-week sprints with clear goals and deliverables
- Daily standups for team sync and blocker resolution
- Sprint planning with stakeholder input
- Sprint retrospectives for continuous improvement

**Comprehensive Documentation**
- Data product documentation in Confluence
- Dashboard usage guides for stakeholders
- Technical documentation for data models
- SOP development for recurring processes

### 5. Data Quality & Reliability
**Elementary Data Quality Framework**
- Implemented dbt tests across all data models
- Created automated alerting for data anomalies
- Established data quality SLAs with stakeholders
- Built monitoring dashboards for data health

**Proactive Monitoring**
- Slack alerts for test failures and anomalies
- Regular data quality reviews with team
- Stakeholder communication protocols for incidents
- Post-incident reviews for continuous improvement

## Key Learnings

### What Worked Well
1. **Early stakeholder alignment** - Building trust from day one was crucial
2. **Agile methodology** - Scrum provided structure while allowing flexibility
3. **Documentation-first approach** - Comprehensive docs reduced onboarding time
4. **Data quality focus** - Proactive monitoring prevented trust issues

### Challenges Overcome
1. **Balancing strategic vs tactical work** - Implemented clear prioritization
2. **Managing stakeholder expectations** - Transparent communication about capacity
3. **Technical debt** - Dedicated time in each sprint for improvements
4. **Cross-team coordination** - Regular syncs and shared documentation

### What I'd Do Differently
1. **Earlier automation** - Could have automated reporting sooner
2. **More proactive data literacy programs** - Would have scaled knowledge sharing
3. **Clearer success metrics** - Would define team KPIs from day one
